# GTA VI Achievement Meme — make.fun bounty entry

Parody of a Rockstar/Xbox achievement toast screen, made for the
make.fun bounty [Post a GTA 6 meme on X](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

- **Post:** https://x.com/scarfacenx/status/2124750978004161021
- **Meme:** `meme.png` (also the post's first picture)
- **Play code in post text:** makefun-wasp-firework-tunnel

## Concept

Two achievement toasts over a Vice City skyline with a blurred "VI" watermark:

- `ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED — Waited 13 Years — Grand Theft Auto VI (10G)`
- `ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED — 47 Bounties Won — while waiting, make.fun (500G)`

The make.fun ghost mascot (from the official press kit) holds the trophy in
the foreground. Punchline: *"still faster than Rockstar."*

## Program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to `/output`,
as required by the bounty.
